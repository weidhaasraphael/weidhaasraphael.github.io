Raphael Weidhaas

Hi!

I am Raphael, a PhD student at Aalto University’s Department of Computer Science, based in Helsinki, Finland. I am part of the research group ‘Humans, Security, and Privacy’.

My research examines how emotions and other psychological factors shape cybersecurity behavior, with a focus on social engineering and related attack vectors. By understanding these factors, I aim to strengthen human resilience to cyber threats. Current work revolves around questions such as:

  • How do emotions affect users’ behavior during social engineering attacks?
  • How do emotions and sociocultural norms shape people’s understanding of security threats?

To answer these questions, I use mixed-methods approaches informed by psychology and human-computer interaction, including interviews, questionnaires, and laboratory observations.

Before starting my PhD, I worked as a course teacher at Aalto University’s School of Arts, Design and Architecture. I was part of the teaching team for the studio course “Computational Methodologies in Landscape Architecture and Urbanism — From Data Points to Spatial Experience”, which focused on experimental and artistic approaches to data interaction and storytelling.
